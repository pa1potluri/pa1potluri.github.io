Free mini-lesson

Flat footage → finished grade

Cameras record flat, low-contrast "log" images to keep detail. Drag the handle to see what the grade does. Left is the flat version, right is the final.

  1. Set black and white points: the silhouette gets real black, the sun gets real white.
  2. Add contrast with a curve, not the contrast slider, so the sky keeps its detail.
  3. Push saturation in the warm range only, so the water and sky glow without the rest turning orange.
  4. Check the silhouette edge: no halo, no lost detail in the sky around the figure.
